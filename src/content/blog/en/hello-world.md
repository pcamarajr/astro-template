## Getting started

This template is built with [Astro 6](https://astro.build) and is part of the [content-stack](https://github.com/pcamarajr/content-stack) ecosystem.

### What's included

- Minimal clean design with custom CSS and design tokens
- i18n support for English and Portuguese (Brazil)
- A blog collection with draft support
- Sample Home and About pages
- Biome for linting and formatting
- Full TypeScript support

### Next steps

1. Update `src/i18n/index.ts` with your site's translations
2. Replace sample content in `src/content/blog/`
3. Customize design tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css`
4. Add content-stack plugins as needed

Happy building.
